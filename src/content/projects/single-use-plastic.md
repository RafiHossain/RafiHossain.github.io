---
title: Single-Use Plastic and Consumer Behaviour in Fultala Market, Khulna
category: academic
order: 15
featured: false
summary: A survey of 50 business owners on plastic consumption and awareness of alternatives at Fultala Market, Khulna.
problem: Khulna generates about 29 tonnes of plastic waste a day, most of it unrecycled, while traders keep using single-use plastic despite knowing its harm.
methods:
  - Structured survey of 50 market businesses
  - Correlation analysis of plastic distribution, cost and availability of alternatives
tools: [Field survey, Statistics]
output: Mean daily plastic use was 0.5 kg per business, highest among vegetable sellers. Plastic distribution and cost showed a moderate inverse correlation.
