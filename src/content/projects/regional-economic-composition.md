---
title: Regional Economic Composition of Rangpur Division
category: academic
order: 12
featured: false
summary: A combined spatial and non-spatial analysis of industrial structure, employment change and inequality across the districts of Rangpur Division.
problem: Economic growth in Bangladesh varies widely between regions, and the largely agricultural Rangpur Division lags behind other divisions.
methods:
  - Location quotient and shift-share analysis
  - National average index, Herfindahl-Hirschman index and entropy index
  - Gini coefficient and Lorenz curve (2011 vs 2022)
  - PCA and AHP to rank development factors
tools: [Spatial analysis, Statistics]
output: Saw mills, rice mills and tailoring are basic industries in Rangpur district. Wage inequality increased between 2011 and 2022, and population was the most important development factor.
