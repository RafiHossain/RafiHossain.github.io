---
title: Site Suitability for Rainwater Harvesting in Drought-Prone Rangpur
category: academic
order: 11
featured: false
summary: A geospatial land suitability analysis to locate sites for rainwater harvesting reservoirs across 58 subdistricts in the drought-prone northwest region of Rangpur, Bangladesh.
problem: Northwest Bangladesh faces drought and scarce arable land, so water storage has to be placed where it is effective without consuming farmland.
methods:
  - Land suitability analysis with eight factors (DEM, land use/land cover, slope, precipitation, NDVI, NDWI, land surface temperature, distance to water)
  - Weighted overlay of remote sensing and GIS layers
tools: [GIS, Remote sensing]
output: Only 6 of 58 subdistricts were found suitable for rainwater harvesting.
