title: Traffic Congestion Modelling Based on Origin and Destination in Khulna
category: academic
order: 10
featured: false
summary: A field study of traffic congestion along the Phultola–Rupsha Ghat corridor of Khulna City, using a congestion index built from intersection counts, traffic volume, spot speed and traffic density.
problem: Congestion in Khulna causes delays and economic losses, worsened by rising vehicle numbers, weak public transport and illegal road occupancy.
methods:
  - Traffic volume, spot speed and density surveys by road segment
  - Congestion index (CI) for origin, sub-origin and destination segments
  - Weekday and weekend comparison
tools: [Field survey]
output: The highest congestion index was 5.79 on weekdays and 4.78 on weekends, both around City Medical and Moylapota Mor. A manuscript from this study has been submitted to Transportation Journal (Wiley).
